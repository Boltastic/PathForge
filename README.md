# 🚀 PathForge

An AI-powered career path finder and roadmap generator.

Find careers that match your:
- skills
- interests
- goals
- lifestyle
- future ambitions

---

## ✨ Features

### 🧠 AI Career Matching
Get personalized career recommendations based on:
- interests
- strengths
- work preferences
- salary goals

---

### 🛣 Personalized Roadmaps
Generate complete learning paths including:
- skills
- certifications
- projects
- timelines

---

### 🤖 AI Replacement Risk Analysis
Understand:
- automation risk
- future demand
- career stability
- AI disruption level

---

### 💰 Salary Simulator
Explore:
- beginner salaries
- senior salaries
- country comparisons
- career growth potential

---

### 📉 Skill Gap Analyzer
Upload resumes or portfolios to discover:
- missing skills
- weak areas
- improvement suggestions

---

### ⚔️ Career Comparison Tool
Compare careers based on:
- income
- work-life balance
- stress
- future demand
- AI safety

---

### 💬 AI Mentor Chat
Ask career questions and receive personalized guidance.

---

### 🎯 Goal Tracking
Track:
- study progress
- milestones
- streaks
- completed projects

---
